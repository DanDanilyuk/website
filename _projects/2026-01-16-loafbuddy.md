## Description

Loaf Buddy is a sourdough baking companion that takes the guesswork out of feeding a starter and mixing a loaf. It pairs two calculators in one interface: a starter feeding planner that sizes your next feed based on when you want it ripe, and a bread calculator that converts baker's percentages into exact gram weights for flour, water, starter, and salt.

## Features

- Paired calculators for starter feeding and bread formulation
- Six timing presets for starter ripeness, from 4-6 hours up to 16-24 hours
- Starter hydration support at 50%, 75%, 100%, and 125%
- Automatic flour, water, and starter calculations from target dough weight
- Flour-specific hydration guidance for All-Purpose, Bread, Whole Wheat, Rye, and Spelt
- Container tare compensation for accurate scale-based measurements
- Dough presets for Bagels, Standard Loaf, Ciabatta, and Focaccia
- Adjustable salt percentage with live recalculation
- Ambient-temperature timing guidance calibrated to 75F / 24C
- Dark mode and a mobile-first layout

## Motivation

Sourdough rewards precision, but the math around baker's percentages, starter hydration, and feed timing is easy to get wrong at 6am on a Saturday. Loaf Buddy automates that math and keeps the schedule honest, so bakers can focus on the dough instead of the spreadsheet.

## Technologies Used

- HTML with embedded CSS and JavaScript
- Baker's percentage calculation algorithms
- Responsive tab-based navigation
- Dark mode theming via CSS

## Impact

Loaf Buddy lowers the barrier for new sourdough bakers while giving experienced ones a faster way to scale a formula. Exact ingredient amounts cut down on waste, and the timing guidance makes it realistic to fit a loaf around a normal week.
